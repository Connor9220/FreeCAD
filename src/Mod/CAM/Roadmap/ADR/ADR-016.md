# ADR-016: The simulator plays each operation in its work plane and turns the stock

## Status
DRAFT

## Context

An **Indexed** **Operation** stores its **Toolpath** in its **Work Plane**'s frame with no rotary words (see ADR-000, **Work Plane**). The simulator must show it the way the machine runs it: the rotary axes turn, the tool points along the **Tool Axis**, and time passes at the programmed feeds.

Options considered:

1. **Simulate the world path** (`getPathWithPlacement()`), with the tool tilted. Simple, but the view does not match a machine whose table turns.
2. **Pass each operation's frame with its path, and turn the stock by the machine's table axes.**

## Decision

Option 2.

- The frame goes in-band with the G-code (`CAMSim.SetFrame`). A move between frames with a different **Tool Axis** is an index, not a cut. Three-axis **Jobs** are unchanged.
- The pose comes from the **Post-Processor**'s own solver, over the **Machine**'s table axes; head axes tilt the tool. A toggle shows the stock fixed instead.
- Time comes from feeds: length divided by **Feed** for each move; an index takes the slowest rotary axis's **Axis Velocity**.
- An index follows the axes' `sequence`: equal values move together.

## Consequences

- Gains: the view matches the machine; cycle time is within about half a second per operation of the estimate.
- Costs: the simulator depends on the **Post-Processor**'s rotation solver.
- Open: the **Post-Processor** ignores `sequence` and writes one `G0 A C` block; the A-axis sign differs between rotary surface and the indexed solver.
