# ADR-013: Lever clamps are placed by a solver

## Status
DRAFT

## Context

A **Lever Clamp** (a T-track hold-down, or a strap clamp on a **Step Block**) presses on the **Stock** at one end, rests on a support at the other, and is held by a bolt or stud between them. Its bolt and nut stand up beside the **Stock**, so it is the most likely workholding to be hit. Its pose depends on the **Stock** height, what the far end rests on, and where the bolt goes into the table.

Options considered:

1. **Baked library files** laid out for one **Stock** height. Simple, but wrong for any other height.
2. **The user places every component.** Full control, many steps, easy to get wrong.
3. **A small solver places it from a few settings.**

## Decision

A solver places it (option 3). The settings are: which end presses (toe or heel), the angle around the press point seen from above, what the far end rests on (table, riser or **Step Block**), and where the bolt goes. The solver places the press point, turns by the angle, then tips the clamp until the pressing end is on the **Stock** and the far end on its support. A bar that would cut the **Stock**'s edge pivots until clear, and says so.

- A lever-clamp library file holds the bar and marked points only (toe contact, heel contact, bolt axis). The bolt length is generated. One library item per hardware set; there is no fastener picker.
- A **Strap Clamp Kit** file holds one of each component for a thread size: step clamps, **Step Blocks**, studs, T-slot nut, flange nut, coupling nut. The solver picks the block and step, or a pair of blocks meshed tooth into tooth, and the shortest stud that reaches through the nut.
- **Step Block** teeth hook the block standing on end: each step's top rises toward its edge. The clamp heel is their exact complement, so pressing down drives it into the corner. The heel seats where its teeth go deepest into the block.
- Edge clamps and toe clamps, which press sideways, stay fixed library files.

## Consequences

- Gains: the clamp follows the real **Stock**; one file serves every height; collisions are checked against where the clamp really is.
- Costs: the solver must understand each clamp type; it caches data read from the file, which must be refreshed when the file changes (ADR-010).
- Open: step-block pitch per kit from measured blocks; tooth proportions; a bolt-position setting; snapping the bolt to the table's T-slots.
